Changes made: two rectangles were cut from the model's base colour and DirectX normal maps
(living bark at x 30, y 560, 200 x 320 px; deadwood at x 1300, y 470, 280 x 380 px, of the
2048 px maps), their edges cross-faded so they tile, resampled to 512 px, and the normal map
converted to the OpenGL convention. See scripts/tile-vermos-bark.py. The model geometry,
foliage textures and other maps are not used.
